USB-OTG Cable Connection Guide

Wired Hardware Backup & Emergency Reverse Phone Power

1

Plug in the OTG Cable

Plug the Type-C male end into your Android phone, and the Micro-USB male end into the ESP32 LoRa module.

2

Reverse Power Sharing (Panel Defense Feature)

Your phone automatically powers the ESP32 LoRa module via OTG! The red power LED on the module will illuminate even if the module's 18650 battery is completely dead.

3

Enable OTG in Phone Settings (If Required)

Brands like Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus, and Xiaomi require turning on OTG manually:

Settings → System / Additional Settings → OTG Connection (ON)

4

Initiate Serial Connection

Tap the button below. When prompted by Android/Browser, allow access to the USB Serial device (115200 baud).
